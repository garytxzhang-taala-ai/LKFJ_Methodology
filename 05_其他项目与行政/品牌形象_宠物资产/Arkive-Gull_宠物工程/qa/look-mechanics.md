Arkive Gull look mechanics

Arkive Gull is a round, soft pixel mascot with a stable lower body and short neck. For look directions, the feet and lower belly stay anchored while the head, beak, sunglasses, eyelids, and upper body carry the direction.

Cardinals:
- 000 up: head tips upward, beak sits slightly higher, sunglasses/eyes aim up, belly and feet stay stable.
- 090 screen-right: head and beak turn toward screen-right, more of the left cheek/body side remains visible, right wing tucks slightly.
- 180 down: head dips down toward the scarf badge, eyelids/sunglasses lower, beak points down.
- 270 screen-left: head and beak turn toward screen-left, opposing the 090 pose, with stable feet and belly.

Motion budget:
Each 22.5 degree step should move the head/beak/sunglasses by a small, even amount. The sprite should not rotate as a whole object. Sunglasses may stay on for the direction rows because they are part of the cool mascot identity; direction must still read through beak position, head angle, and silhouette.
